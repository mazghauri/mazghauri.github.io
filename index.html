I am a mechanical engineer working where heat transfer, fluid mechanics and scientific machine learning meet. Much of my research uses Lie symmetry analysis to reduce the equations of non-Newtonian, magnetohydrodynamic and boundary-layer flows to forms that can be solved exactly, and then tests those solutions against physics-informed neural networks, the Deep Galerkin Method and spectral solvers.

Alongside this analytical work I have run experiments on flow-induced piezoelectric energy harvesters, field-tested cooling for solar panels, and written reviews on green hydrogen, maritime biofuels and data center cooling. I now model data center cooling from chip to facility as a Thermal Engineer at Sinkpath.

Outside the lab I have led publications teams for ASME, IMechE and NUST university events, and completed community service projects teaching engineering to young girls and running a Ramadan iftar drive.
